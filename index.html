Why field verification matters

The model score is a susceptibility estimate learned from terrain and historical-inventory features — it is not a confirmed ground observation. Field reports let officers and citizens verify conditions on the ground before any response is dispatched.

How to read the risk score

The risk score shown throughout this platform is a relative susceptibility score learned from historical patterns — it is not a scientifically validated landslide probability, and the model does not predict the occurrence, timing, or exact location of a specific future landslide. It should be used alongside official advisories and field verification, not in place of them.
